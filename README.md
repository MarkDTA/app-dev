### Breaking Bad is different compared to other shows. It’s not just a show, it’s like watching this slow, crazy transformation that feels way too real. Walt starts off as this awkward, struggling teacher, who is seen like a pushover, and as the story goes, we can see how he builds up his drug empire through his' and other's blood and sweat. Every episode doesn't feel like a filler, everything is building up the main story or has some commentary about the characters. Overall, a very good watch, the start may be slow to some people but it's worth the wait.
